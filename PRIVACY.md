# Privacy

The `reel-in` Google OAuth app is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uses rclone on the owner's own Mac to move files from the owner's Google Drive `Footage Inbox` folder to local disk.

- No data is collected, stored, or shared with anyone else.
- Credentials stay in rclone's local config on the owner's machine.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
